NEW GAME

You start with cash in Swiss francs, a job and today's real market prices. Every month your salary comes in and your rent and bills go out. Buy and sell stocks, ETFs, crypto, metals, cars, watches, art and homes, then come back every day to see how your decisions did. Swiss rules apply: capital gains are tax free, stock and ETF trades pay stamp duty, and wealth tax is due on 31 December with your city's tariff. You can also day trade at simulated live prices while each market is open: close the trade before the market closes, or it is reverted.

Game only, not tax advice. Salaries, income and wealth tax, rents and living costs are rough averages for each city (single person, no church tax). Market prices are real daily closes from Randata, converted to CHF with that day's exchange rate. Dividends are paid to you in cash. Car, watch, art and house prices are simulated around real prices, the same for every player. Day trading prices are simulated too: a random walk from the last real close, twice as lively as the real market, the same for every player. Markets keep their regular hours, without holidays or lunch breaks.
