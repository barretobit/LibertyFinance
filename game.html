NEW GAME

You start with cash in Swiss francs, a job and today's real market prices. Every month your salary comes in and your rent and bills go out. Buy and sell stocks, ETFs, crypto, metals, cars, watches, art and homes, then come back every day to see how your decisions did. Swiss rules apply: capital gains are tax free, stock and ETF trades pay stamp duty, and wealth tax is due on 31 December with your city's tariff.
