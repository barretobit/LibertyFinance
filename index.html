HOW IT WORKS
LIBERTY FINANCE IS A PERSONAL WEALTH MANAGEMENT SYSTEM THAT RUNS ENTIRELY IN YOUR BROWSER. IT TRACKS YOUR FULL FINANCIAL PICTURE — WHAT YOU OWN, WHAT YOU EARN, WHAT YOU SPEND AND WHERE YOUR MONEY GROWS — ALL IN ONE PLACE.
YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

Everything is stored as plain JSON files on your machine (or in your browser’s private storage). No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. You can create multiple profiles to keep separate books — for example personal, business or family.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH THE APP
  • DASHBOARD — live net worth, liquid net worth, investment performance, a 12-month forecast, yearly performance and recent transactions.
  • PORTFOLIOS — group your accounts (Retirement, Growth, Emergency…) and track value, P&L and allocation per portfolio.
  • ACCOUNTS — cash, investments, retirement funds, precious metals and tangible assets, each with currency, custodian, deposits/withdrawals and a full transaction history.
  • INCOMES / EXPENSES — log what you earn and spend to see average monthly income, monthly & annual costs and what you can save.
  • GOALS — set savings targets with priorities and automatic funding from your accounts.
  • DEBTS — track what you owe and what is owed to you.
  • CUSTODIANS — the banks, brokers and vaults that hold your assets.
  • EXCHANGE RATES & PRECIOUS METALS — date-based FX rates and live gold / silver / platinum / palladium spot prices.
FIRST STEPS AFTER CREATING YOUR PROFILE

Once you create a profile and enter the app, follow these steps in order:

1
REGISTER YOUR CUSTODIANS

Open the CUSTODIANS page and add every institution that holds your money. What is a Custodian? Put simply, it is the company that keeps your assets safe on your behalf — usually a bank or broker (e.g. UBS, Interactive Brokers), or a vault that stores your gold. Even when you trade through an app, the actual money and securities sit at a custodian. Registering them keeps your accounts organized and easy to understand.

2
REGISTER YOUR INCOMES AND EXPENSES

Log your income sources (salary, freelance, rent) under INCOMES, and your recurring costs (rent, insurance, groceries) under EXPENSES. The app then calculates your average monthly income, your monthly and annual expenses, and ultimately what you can save and invest.

3
CREATE PORTFOLIOS AND PUT ACCOUNTS INSIDE

Go to PORTFOLIOS and create a portfolio — a group that holds related accounts, like “Retirement”, “Growth” or “Emergency Fund”. Then open the portfolio and press + ADD ACCOUNT to place your bank and broker accounts inside it. For each account you choose a custodian, a currency and a type (Cash, Investment, Retirement Fund, Precious Metal or Tangible Asset), and set its starting value and date.

4
KEEP IT UPDATED

Record deposits, withdrawals and valuations on each account to keep balances and performance accurate. Your dashboard then computes net worth, P&L and a 12-month forecast automatically.
